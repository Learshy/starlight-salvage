Starlight Salvage — a wave-based arena shooter: fly your skiff through a debris field, blast asteroids, collect salvage, pick 1 of 3 upgrades between waves, and survive all 10 waves.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. WASD/arrows to fly, mouse to aim, click or SPACE to fire, P to pause, M to mute, R to restart.
